# Contributing to Couchkit

Thank you for helping. Small, focused changes are easiest to review.

- **Bugs and ideas:** open an issue on GitHub. For a bug, say what you pressed, what you expected, what happened, your system (macOS or Windows) and your TV model.
- **Code changes:** open a pull request. Run `npm run typecheck` and `npm test` first, and add a test for new behaviour.
- **Safety first:** anything that changes the TV must be reversible or ask for confirmation, and commands sent to the TV come from fixed lists in the code, never from text the user types (except the Console page, which says so).
- **No copied code:** only submit work you wrote or have the right to share under Apache-2.0. Do not copy code, text or assets from other tools.
- **Licence of your contribution:** by submitting a change you agree it is licensed under the Apache License 2.0, like the rest of the project (Apache License section 5). You keep the copyright in what you wrote, and you will be credited in `AUTHORS` if you wish.
- **Name and logo:** the Couchkit name and artwork are not covered by that licence (see `TRADEMARKS.md`).
